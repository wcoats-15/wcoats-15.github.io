Summer Research — Tennessee Tech University

VTOL Aircraft Research

I completed summer research at Tennessee Tech University working on a VTOL plane. My work involved a multi-propulsion testing rig and engineering questions surrounding motor and battery placement, center of gravity, torque, yaw authority, pitch behavior, weight distribution, and structural design.

This experience gave me the opportunity to apply physics to a real aerospace system where propulsion, stability, mechanical layout, and structural constraints all interact.
